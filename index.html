E-commerce AI

BuyRight: "Will this work for me?"

Many returns of appliances, electronics and home goods are products that work but don't suit the buyer's room, phone or space. BuyRight asks 2 to 5 quick questions on the product page, gives a clear verdict from written rules (will a 1-ton AC cool this room, will this charger fast-charge this phone, will this rack fit this wall), explains it with citations to the specs and to reviews from owners in a similar situation, and suggests products that do fit.

  • Mismatch returns: −25% target, A/B-test ready
