CloudBill Detective

Cost tools tell you the cloud bill went up. CloudBill Detective tells you which change caused it, who owns it, and the fix to approve. It joins AWS billing with the change log (deploys, Terraform, CloudTrail) to explain every sustained cost change with evidence, splits usage from price, routes each case to an owner and drafts a read-only fix for human approval. Every number in AI-written text is fact-checked, and 12 launch evals run as a build gate. Full AI PM package: user research, AI opportunity map, PRD with North Star metric, cost model and deck.
